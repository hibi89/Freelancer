**KRW 500k–600k+ per month** in actual *received* freelance/remote income, not job posting potential or theoretical billable hours. Pursue the first small paid contract, then repeatable delivery. ## Guardrails
- Do not auto-buy Connects / subscribe to Freelancer Plus / purchase API access.
- Avoid projects requiring undisclosed chatbot impersonation, forbidden outsourced AI evaluation, private PII exposure, policy violations or impossible turnaround.
- Every advertised revenue figure needs direct evidence of deposit and fees; separate gross, platform fee, transfer costs, taxes and net.
- Benchmark $17/hr as initial Upwork profile price, allow per-proposal adjustment.
- Never promise finding instant work or a monthly minimum by a set date.
